Privacy Notice

Barangay Minante 1 Integrated Management System

Information collected

The system collects the personal, residency, emergency-contact, account, and identity-document type information you provide for resident verification and delivery of authorized Barangay services.

If you are the head of the family, you may provide family members' names, dates of birth, and relationships. Family members are not given login accounts. Disability details and LGBTQIA+ self-identification are optional. LGBTQIA+ answers and categories are recorded only with the resident's separate consent.

How information is used

Authorized Barangay personnel use this information to review account eligibility, process service requests, communicate status updates, maintain audit records, and meet lawful administrative responsibilities. Reports may include aggregate LGBTQIA+ response and category counts for active approved profiles; reports do not show resident names or individual answers.

Protection and access

Access is role-restricted. Sensitive profile and blotter information is not displayed publicly. LGBTQIA+ answers and categories are not shown as individual records in reports.

Your consent

Registration records whether you agreed to this notice and the date and time of consent. Contact Barangay Minante 1 for questions or requests concerning your information.
